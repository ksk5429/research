I work on offshore wind turbine foundations and how to monitor them: how scour changes the natural frequency of a turbine on a tripod suction-bucket foundation, how long-term vibration monitoring can track foundation stiffness, how foundations fail in uplift, studied by centrifuge tests and image-based measurement, and how numerical limit analysis and digital twins can support these questions.

Detailed status of every paper and research line, the literature synthesis and the writing workflow are on research notes.

Research themes

  • Scour and natural frequency of offshore wind turbines. Centrifuge tests and calibrated numerical models of a 4.2 MW turbine on a tripod suction-bucket foundation.
  • Structural health monitoring. Correcting measured natural frequencies for operating and environmental conditions, and a Kalman-filter digital twin for tracking foundation stiffness.
  • Uplift of foundations. Progressive failure of inverted-T foundations on level and sloping ground, observed by particle image velocimetry (PIV) in the centrifuge and studied numerically.
  • Analysis and measurement software. K-ponytail, a finite element limit analysis program, and K-DIC, a digital image correlation program for laboratory measurements; both in development.
